First Aid Guide

General, step-by-step information for common situations. In an emergency, always call your local emergency number first.

Minor Cuts & Scrapes

Clean gently, apply pressure to stop bleeding, and cover with a clean bandage.

Minor Burns

Cool the area under running water and cover loosely — seek care for large or severe burns.

Sprains

Rest, ice, gentle compression, and elevation can help with mild sprains.

Fainting

Lay the person down and raise their legs; seek help if they do not recover quickly.

Serious emergencies

For chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, or loss of consciousness — call emergency services immediately. Do not rely on this page.
