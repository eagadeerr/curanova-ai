About CuraNova AI

Our mission and how we approach health information responsibly.

CuraNova AI was built to make general health information easier to access and understand — clearly explained, always paired with honest guidance about when to see a real doctor.

We are not a hospital, clinic, or telemedicine provider. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicine, or replace professional care. Our goal is simple: help people feel a little more informed before, during, and after they talk to a licensed healthcare professional.

Every response from CuraNova AI is educational in nature, and clearly flags when a situation may need urgent, real-world medical attention.
